Find Internships for Students

Internships help you gain real-world experience and build your resume. Explore these platforms to find internships in software development, data analytics, cybersecurity, and more.

Internship Portals

  • Internshala: Browse thousands of internships across India
  • LinkedIn Internships: Search internships by location and field
  • LetsIntern: Find online and offline internships
  • Unstop (formerly Dare2Compete): Explore internship opportunities

How to Get Selected

  • Apply early—many internships fill up quickly
  • Tailor your resume to match the internship requirements
  • Highlight relevant projects from your B.Tech coursework
  • Prepare for technical assessments and coding challenges
