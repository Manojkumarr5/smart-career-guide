Find Jobs for Fresh Graduates

Looking for entry-level jobs and fresher positions? Use the resources below to find opportunities that match your engineering branch and skills.

Job Portals

  • LinkedIn Jobs: Search fresher and entry-level jobs
  • Naukri: Browse fresher jobs
  • Internshala Jobs: Explore job listings
  • Indeed India: Search jobs by location and skills

Tips for Freshers

  • Update your LinkedIn profile with projects and skills
  • Prepare a strong resume highlighting your B.Tech projects
  • Apply to 5-10 jobs daily to increase your chances
  • Practice common interview questions for technical roles
